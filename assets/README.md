# Spideyhood Asset Directory

This directory is ready for your custom cartoon assets! When you provide your sprite sheets, textures, or audio clips, you can drop them into the appropriate subfolders below:

## Folder Structure

- `assets/images/player/`
  - Spidey character animations (idle, run, jump, swing, punch, hurt).
  - Can be single frames or PNG sprite sheets.
- `assets/images/enemies/`
  - Thugs and villain cartoon sprite sheets.
- `assets/images/comic_fx/`
  - Custom comic pop-ups (*"POW!"*, *"THWIP!"*, *"BAM!"*, *"K.O.!"*), starbursts, comic action lines, or speech bubbles.
- `assets/images/environment/`
  - Rooftops, brick textures, streetlamps, billboards, and background layers.
- `assets/images/ui/`
  - Custom health bars, comic buttons, frame borders, and victory banners.
- `assets/audio/sfx/`
  - Sound effects for web-shots, punches, jumps, and victory stingers.
- `assets/audio/bgm/`
  - Background music tracks for neighborhood rooftop patrol.
